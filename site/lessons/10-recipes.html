Lesson 10

Five patches to build

Time to put the pieces together. Each recipe below is a complete Virtual Analog patch with every setting given. Play it here, send it to your FM-1 in one click, or, better, build it by hand from a blank preset so the steps stick.

Building from a blank preset

Making each patch by hand is where the learning happens. Start each one the same way:

  1. Pick a preset slot you don’t mind overwriting, then press EDIT, turn SELECT to Erase Preset and press SEL, turn SELECT to Erase and press SEL, then turn SELECT to VA and press SEL.
  2. Hold ENV for a second to switch the Envelope on, then press ENV and raise Sustain, or every note will be silent after its attack.
  3. Work through the recipe’s settings top to bottom: Oscillator and Filter under EDIT, the envelope under ENV, the LFO under LFO, effects under FX.
  4. Name it (Name, near the end of the EDIT list) and press SAVE.

Or, with MIDI connected, select the blank VA preset and press Send settings to FM-1 on a recipe, then compare. The page sends the oscillator and filter settings, the envelope, the LFO’s Speed, Pitch Mod Depth and Delay, and the four effects’ main settings. A few things have no MIDI control and must be set by hand: the LFO’s Wave, Mono, Glide Time and the distortion type. Cutoff is approximate, since how the FM-1 maps MIDI values onto 20 Hz to 20 kHz isn’t documented, so trust your ears.

1. Warm pad

Slow to arrive, slow to leave, gently moving. The detuned saws give width; the closed filter and a slow LFO on the cutoff keep it soft and alive; chorus and a hall reverb put it in a room.

By hand: Saw · Super 55 · Detune 40 · Drift 15 · Filter LP24, Cutoff about 1.8 kHz, Resonance 15, Envelope 15, Decay 60, Key Tracking 33 · LFO to Cutoff 14 · Envelope on: Attack 55, Decay 60, Sustain 85, Release 85 · LFO Triangle, Speed 22 · Chorus on (Rate 25, Depth 45, Mix 45) · Reverb Hall (Decay 60, Mix 40).

Make it yours: raise Detune for more shimmer, or lower Cutoff for a darker, more distant pad.

2. Supersaw lead

The trance lead from lesson 6: lots of Super, Detune in its upper range, a quick attack, a touch of delayed vibrato and some echo.

By hand: Saw · Super 85 · Detune 62 · Drift 10 · Filter LP24, Cutoff about 6.5 kHz, Resonance 10 · Envelope on: Attack 3, Decay 50, Sustain 90, Release 35 · LFO Sine, Speed 55, Pitch Mod Depth 12, Delay 45 · Delay on (Rate 55, Feedback 30, Mix 22) · Reverb Plate (Decay 40, Mix 25).

Make it yours: put Super and Detune on KNOB3 and KNOB4 and ride them during a phrase.

3. Acid bass

One saw, a resonant low-pass held mostly shut, and a short filter envelope that snaps it open on every note. Mono with glide makes notes slide into each other when you play legato; a little distortion adds grit.

By hand: Saw · Super 0 · Filter LP24, Cutoff about 320 Hz, Resonance 72, Envelope 68, Decay 32, Velocity 40, Key Tracking 33 · Envelope on: Attack 0, Decay 40, Sustain 70, Release 10 · Mono on, then Glide Time about 30 under GLO › Glide (Glide Time belongs to the whole FM-1, so it isn’t saved with this preset) · Distortion Soft Clip (Gain 45, Tone 50, Level 60).

Make it yours: play it from the FM-1’s sequencer and keep one hand on KNOB1 (Cutoff) and the other on KNOB2 (Resonance).

4. Pluck

No sustain at all: every note is a short, bright snap that darkens as it fades, like a plucked string. The filter envelope does most of the work. A dotted echo turns simple patterns into something hypnotic.

By hand: Saw · Sub 20 · Filter LP24, Cutoff about 550 Hz, Resonance 28, Envelope 75, Decay 24 · Envelope on: Attack 0, Decay 35, Sustain 0, Release 30 · Delay on (Rate 45, Feedback 35, Mix 30) · Reverb Room (Decay 35, Mix 20).

Make it yours: switch the waveform to Square for a woodier, marimba-like pluck.

5. Ambient drone

An atmosphere that never quite repeats. Detuned triangles with plenty of Drift wander against each other; a slow LFO sweeps a resonant filter; a little noise adds air; long delay and a big hall smear it into a space.

By hand: Tri · Super 60 · Detune 30 · Drift 45 · Noise 12 · Filter LP12, Cutoff about 1.4 kHz, Resonance 38 · LFO to Cutoff 30 · Envelope on: Attack 80, Decay 60, Sustain 100, Release 100 · LFO Sine, Speed 12 · Chorus on (Rate 15, Depth 60, Mix 50) · Delay on (Rate 15, Feedback 55, Mix 30) · Reverb Hall (Decay 85, Mix 55).

Make it yours: switch on the FM-1’s arpeggiator with Latch on, hold a chord, and let the drone play itself while both hands work the knobs.

These settings are written in the FM-1’s own units (mostly 0 to 100, with Cutoff in hertz), so they transfer row for row. The page’s synth is a model, not a copy, so a patch will sound related on both, not identical. That’s normal: tune it by ear on the hardware.
