Learn synthesis by ear and by eye.

A free course built around the M-VAVE FM-1 and Baud Girl’s FM-1+VA firmware. Every idea comes with something to play: the page synthesizes it, draws it, and, if your FM-1 is plugged in, sets the same controls on the hardware so you can hear the real thing side by side.
